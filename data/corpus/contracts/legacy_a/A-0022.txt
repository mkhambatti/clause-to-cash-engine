Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 7th September 2025 between Arden Data Services, LLC ("we", "us") and Meridian Pictures Corporation ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: describing and tagging titles so viewers can find them.

The relationship runs for four years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 180 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 100,000 title cost $14.96 each, the next 210,000 title cost $13.93 each, and anything beyond 310,000 title costs $13.22 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least $880,000 a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices are locked for the whole relationship.

Invoices are due 60 days after receipt.

Only the Customer itself may use this arrangement; its sister companies cannot.

The contract cannot be transferred, including to an acquirer, unless the other side agrees.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

Delaware law governs.

Signed by both sides.
