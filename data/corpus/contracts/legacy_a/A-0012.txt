Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 17th September 2023 between Ingram Messaging Corporation ("we", "us") and Meridian Streaming LLC ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: nudging subscribers with alerts and newsletters.

The relationship runs for two years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 180 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 15,000 million-messages cost $100.51 each, the next 31,000 million-messages cost $97.29 each, and anything beyond 46,000 million-messages costs $94.20 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$1.15 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices are locked for the whole relationship.

Invoices are due 60 days after receipt.

Only the Customer itself may use this arrangement; its sister companies cannot.

The contract binds both sides and whoever follows them.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We promise the prices here are as good as any comparable client gets.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

Delaware law governs.

Signed by both sides.
