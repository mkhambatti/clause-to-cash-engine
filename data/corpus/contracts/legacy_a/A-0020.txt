Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 5th April 2021 between Northgate Subtitle Services Corp. ("we", "us") and Meridian Studios, Inc. ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: on-screen translations of dialogue.

The relationship runs for three years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 180 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another two years.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down with volume, applied to everything: up to 320,000 finished-minute a year each finished-minute costs $8.77; once volume passes 320,000 every finished-minute is repriced at $8.30; past 970,000 it drops to $7.87 for every finished-minute.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$1.51 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices go up 5% every year on the anniversary.

Invoices are due 30 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can only buy under this deal if we agree first.

If either side is acquired or merges, the contract simply moves to the new owner.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

New York law governs.

Signed by both sides.
