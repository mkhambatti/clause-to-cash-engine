Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 20th June 2020 between Solstice Enterprise Analytics Limited ("we", "us") and Meridian Studios, Inc. ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: the finance and procurement system of record, hosted by the vendor.

The relationship runs for three years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 90 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

The Customer can walk away early on 180 days' notice, but owes three quarters of what is left on the commitment.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 71,000 user-month cost $99.48 each, the next 139,000 user-month cost $93.37 each, and anything beyond 210,000 user-month costs $89.67 each.

Invoices are due 30 days after receipt.

If either side is acquired or merges, the contract simply moves to the new owner.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

New York law governs.

Signed by both sides.

Side Letter 1 - dated 6th April 2021

The parties agree to add two years to the initial period.

Side Letter 2 - dated 19th March 2022

Replacing the pricing above, from now on: the first 71,000 user-month cost $91.17 each, the next 139,000 user-month cost $85.57 each, and anything beyond 210,000 user-month costs $82.18 each.
