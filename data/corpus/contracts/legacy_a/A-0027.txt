MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of July 5, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Meridian Studios, Inc. ("Customer") and Arden People Systems LLC, a California corporation ("Vendor").

1. Services. Vendor shall provide human resources and payroll software (the "Services") to Customer in accordance with this Agreement.

2.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months each, unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least three (3) months prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Termination for Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice, subject to payment of an early termination fee equal to seventy-five percent (75%) of the fees that would have been payable for the remainder of the then-current term.

3.1 Fees. Customer shall pay the fees set forth in Schedule A.

3.2 Fee Adjustments. Fees are fixed for the Term.

3.3 Minimum Commitment. Customer commits to purchase not less than $1,770,000 of Services in each contract year (the "Annual Minimum Commitment"). Any shortfall will be invoiced at the end of the contract year.

3.4 Payment Terms. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.1 Affiliates. Customer's Affiliates may purchase Services under this Agreement at the rates set forth in Schedule A, and all such purchases will count toward Annual Volume.

5.1 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent. If Customer undergoes a change of control, Vendor may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

6.1 Exclusivity. Nothing in this Agreement obligates Customer to purchase the Services exclusively from Vendor.

6.2 Pricing Protection. Vendor makes no representation regarding prices charged to its other customers.

7. Confidentiality. Each party's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

7. Indemnification. Vendor will indemnify Customer, its Affiliates, and their respective officers and directors against third-party claims arising from Vendor's breach of this Agreement.

7. Limitation of Liability. Each party's total liability will not exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This Section states each party's exclusive remedy for such claims.

7. Service Levels. Vendor will maintain 99.9% availability and will issue service credits of ten percent (10%) of the monthly fees for any month in which availability falls below 99.5%.

7. Notices. Notices are deemed given five (5) days after mailing.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, without regard to conflict of law principles.

SCHEDULE A - PRICING
Unit: employee-month
Tier 1: 0 to 210,000 employee-month per year at $12.97 per employee-month
Tier 2: 210,000 to 620,000 employee-month per year at $11.95 per employee-month
Tier 3: Above 620,000 employee-month per year at $11.56 per employee-month
Tiered pricing applies on a graduated basis: each unit is charged at the rate of the tier in which it falls.
