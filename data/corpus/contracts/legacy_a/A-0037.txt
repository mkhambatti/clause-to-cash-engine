Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 29th March 2021 between Marlow Protective Services Inc. ("we", "us") and Meridian Studios, Inc. ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: uniformed officers at our lots and offices.

The relationship runs for two years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 90 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another two years.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 100,000 guard-hour cost $38.44 each, the next 210,000 guard-hour cost $36.07 each, and anything beyond 310,000 guard-hour costs $34.36 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$4.85 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Invoices are due 45 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can also buy at these rates, and their volume counts toward the tiers.

The contract cannot be transferred, including to an acquirer, unless the other side agrees.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

California law governs.

Signed by both sides.
