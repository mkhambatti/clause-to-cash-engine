MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of November 10, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Meridian Studios, Inc. ("Customer") and Solstice Workplace Corp., a California corporation ("Vendor").

1. Services. Vendor shall provide productivity and endpoint software licenses (the "Services") to Customer in accordance with this Agreement.

2.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months each, unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Termination for Convenience. Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.

3.1 Fees. Customer shall pay the fees set forth in Schedule A.

3.2 Fee Adjustments. Fees are fixed for the Term.

3.3 Minimum Commitment. Customer commits to purchase not less than $8,240,000 of Services in each contract year (the "Annual Minimum Commitment"). Any shortfall will be invoiced at the end of the contract year.

3.4 Payment Terms. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.1 Affiliates. Customer's Affiliates may purchase Services under this Agreement at the rates set forth in Schedule A, and all such purchases will count toward Annual Volume.

5.1 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement, whether by operation of law, change of control, or otherwise, without the other party's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

6.1 Exclusivity. During the Term, Customer will purchase all of its requirements for the Services exclusively from Vendor.

6.2 Pricing Protection. Vendor makes no representation regarding prices charged to its other customers.

7. Confidentiality. Each party's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

7. Indemnification. Vendor will indemnify Customer, its Affiliates, and their respective officers and directors against third-party claims arising from Vendor's breach of this Agreement.

7. Limitation of Liability. Each party's total liability will not exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This Section states each party's exclusive remedy for such claims.

7. Service Levels. Vendor will maintain 99.9% availability and will issue service credits of ten percent (10%) of the monthly fees for any month in which availability falls below 99.5%.

7. Notices. Notices are deemed given five (5) days after mailing.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.

SCHEDULE A - PRICING
Unit: user-month
Tier 1: 0 to 290,000 user-month per year at $28.52 per user-month
Tier 2: 290,000 to 880,000 user-month per year at $26.58 per user-month
Tier 3: Above 880,000 user-month per year at $25.56 per user-month
Tiered pricing applies on an all-units basis: the rate for the tier in which total Annual Volume falls applies to all units.
