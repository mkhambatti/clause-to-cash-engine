Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 3rd July 2025 between Arden People Systems, Inc. ("we", "us") and Meridian Pictures Corporation ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: software that runs paychecks and employee records.

The relationship runs for two years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least six months before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 210,000 employee-month cost $12.97 each, the next 410,000 employee-month cost $11.95 each, and anything beyond 620,000 employee-month costs $11.56 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$2.24 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices go up 5% every year on the anniversary.

Invoices are due 60 days after receipt.

Only the Customer itself may use this arrangement; its sister companies cannot.

If either side is acquired or merges, the contract simply moves to the new owner.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

Delaware law governs.

Signed by both sides.
