Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 17th January 2024 between Tidewater Shield Technologies Corporation ("we", "us") and Meridian Pictures Corporation ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: issuing the keys that let paying viewers decrypt protected video.

The relationship runs for one year counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 30 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

The Customer can walk away early on 180 days' notice, but owes all of what is left on the commitment.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 13,000 million-licenses cost $248.39 each, the next 25,000 million-licenses cost $228.89 each, and anything beyond 38,000 million-licenses costs $218.50 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$2.97 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices go up 3% every year on the anniversary.

Invoices are due 30 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can also buy at these rates, and their volume counts toward the tiers.

The contract cannot be transferred, including to an acquirer, unless the other side agrees.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

Delaware law governs.

Signed by both sides.
