Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 16th July 2025 between Opaline Language Studios, LLC ("we", "us") and Meridian Streaming LLC ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: re-voicing shows into other languages.

The relationship runs for one year counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 60 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 91,000 finished-minute cost $85.59 each, the next 179,000 finished-minute cost $82.94 each, and anything beyond 270,000 finished-minute costs $76.89 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$10.98 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices are locked for the whole relationship.

Invoices are due 60 days after receipt.

Only the Customer itself may use this arrangement; its sister companies cannot.

The contract binds both sides and whoever follows them.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

New York law governs.

Signed by both sides.
