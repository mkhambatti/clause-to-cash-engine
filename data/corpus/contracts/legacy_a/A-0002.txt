Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 24th July 2021 between Arden Archive Systems LLC ("we", "us") and Meridian Streaming LLC ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: somewhere to keep petabytes of masters and backups.

The relationship runs for two years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 90 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 410 PB-month cost $21,952 each, the next 790 PB-month cost $21,007 each, and anything beyond 1,200 PB-month costs $19,992 each.

There is no spend floor.

Prices are locked for the whole relationship.

Invoices are due 45 days after receipt.

Only the Customer itself may use this arrangement; its sister companies cannot.

The contract cannot be transferred, including to an acquirer, unless the other side agrees.

We promise the prices here are as good as any comparable client gets.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

California law governs.

Signed by both sides.
