MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of January 15, 2022 (the "Effective Date") by and between Meridian Pictures Corporation ("Customer") and Hollis Render Cloud, Inc., a California corporation ("Vendor").

1. Services. Vendor shall provide visual effects rendering services (the "Services") to Customer in accordance with this Agreement.

2.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months each, unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least three (3) months prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Termination for Convenience. Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.

3.1 Fees. Customer shall pay the fees set forth in Schedule A.

3.2 Fee Adjustments. Fees will increase by five percent (5%) on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

3.3 Minimum Commitment. Customer commits to purchase not less than $6,330,000 of Services in each contract year (the "Annual Minimum Commitment"). Any shortfall will be invoiced at the end of the contract year.

3.4 Payment Terms. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days of receipt.

4.1 Affiliates. The Services are for Customer's internal use only, and no Affiliate or third party may use or purchase the Services under this Agreement.

5.1 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement, whether by operation of law, change of control, or otherwise, without the other party's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

6.1 Exclusivity. Nothing in this Agreement obligates Customer to purchase the Services exclusively from Vendor.

6.2 Pricing Protection. Vendor represents that the unit prices in Schedule A are no less favorable than those offered to any similarly situated customer purchasing comparable volumes.

7. Confidentiality. Each party's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

7. Indemnification. Vendor will indemnify Customer, its Affiliates, and their respective officers and directors against third-party claims arising from Vendor's breach of this Agreement.

7. Limitation of Liability. Each party's total liability will not exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This Section states each party's exclusive remedy for such claims.

7. Service Levels. Vendor will maintain 99.9% availability and will issue service credits of ten percent (10%) of the monthly fees for any month in which availability falls below 99.5%.

7. Notices. Notices are deemed given five (5) days after mailing.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.

SCHEDULE A - PRICING
Unit: core-hour
Tier 1: 0 to 200,000,000 core-hour per year at $0.0667 per core-hour
Tier 2: 200,000,000 to 590,000,000 core-hour per year at $0.0635 per core-hour
Tier 3: Above 590,000,000 core-hour per year at $0.0601 per core-hour
Tiered pricing applies on a graduated basis: each unit is charged at the rate of the tier in which it falls.
