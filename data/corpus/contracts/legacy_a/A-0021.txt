Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 18th February 2023 between Juniper Data Services, LLC ("we", "us") and Meridian Pictures Corporation ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: describing and tagging titles so viewers can find them.

The relationship runs for four years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 60 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down with volume, applied to everything: up to 100,000 title a year each title costs $14.63; once volume passes 100,000 every title is repriced at $13.90; past 310,000 it drops to $13.44 for every title.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$1.18 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices go up 2% every year on the anniversary.

Invoices are due 60 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can also buy at these rates, and their volume counts toward the tiers.

If either side is acquired or merges, the contract simply moves to the new owner.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

Delaware law governs.

Signed by both sides.
