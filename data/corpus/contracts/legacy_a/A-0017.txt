Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 9th April 2026 between Tidewater Caption Services LLC ("we", "us") and Meridian Streaming LLC ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: producing on-screen text for the hearing impaired and checking it.

The relationship runs for three years counting from signature.

There is no evergreen: if the parties want to keep going after the initial period they will have to sign something new.

The Customer can walk away early on 30 days' notice, but owes half of what is left on the commitment.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 14,000 program-hour cost $137.03 each, the next 27,000 program-hour cost $132.27 each, and anything beyond 41,000 program-hour costs $122.16 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$3.78 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices are locked for the whole relationship.

Invoices are due 45 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can also buy at these rates, and their volume counts toward the tiers.

The contract cannot be transferred, including to an acquirer, unless the other side agrees.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

California law governs.

Signed by both sides.
