MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into
effective as of the 6th day of July, 2020 (the "Effective Date"), by and
between Ingram Messaging Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Vendor"), and
Meridian Studios, Inc. ("Customer").

WHEREAS, Vendor is in the business of providing customer messaging delivery
services; and WHEREAS, Customer wishes to obtain such services; NOW,
THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

3.1 Initial Term. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective
Date and, unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, shall
continue for a period of two (2) years (the "Initial Term").

3.2 Renewal. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall be automatically
extended for successive periods of one (1) year (each, a "Renewal Term")
unless either Party gives the other written notice of its intention not to
renew not less than ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-
current term.

3.3 Termination Without Cause. Customer shall have the right, exercisable at
any time upon not less than thirty (30) days' written notice, to terminate
this Agreement without cause; provided that Customer shall pay Vendor an early
termination charge equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the fees payable for
the balance of the then-current term.

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in Schedule A.

4.2 Minimum Fees. Customer shall pay Vendor annual minimum fees of USD
1,940,000 irrespective of actual usage (the "Minimum Commitment"), on a take-
or-pay basis.

4.3 Adjustments. Fees shall be adjusted annually, effective on each
anniversary of the Effective Date, by an increase of three percent (3%) over
the fees in effect for the preceding year.

4.4 Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days after
receipt thereof.

6.1 Successors. This Agreement shall be binding upon and shall inure to the
benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

6.3 Pricing Warranty. Vendor makes no representation or warranty regarding the
prices charged to other customers.

7.1 The obligations of confidentiality set forth herein shall survive for a
period of three (3) years following termination or expiration of this
Agreement.

7.2 Vendor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates
and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against any
third-party claims arising out of Vendor's breach hereof.

7.3 In no event shall either Party's aggregate liability exceed the fees paid
in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to
liability, which shall be the Parties' exclusive remedy.

7.4 Vendor shall maintain availability of 99.9% and shall credit Customer ten
percent (10%) of the monthly fees for each month in which availability is
below 99.5%.

7.5 Any notice shall be deemed given five (5) days after deposit in the mail.

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of the State of California, without giving effect to
conflict of laws principles.

SCHEDULE A. PRICING. For Annual Volume of up to 15,000 million-messages, the
Unit Price shall be $100.51 per million-messages; for Annual Volume in excess
of 15,000 million-messages and up to 46,000 million-messages, the Unit Price
shall be $97.29 per million-messages; and for Annual Volume in excess of
46,000 million-messages, the Unit Price shall be $94.20 per million-messages.
The foregoing tiered Unit Prices shall be applied on a marginal basis, such
that each unit of Annual Volume shall be priced at the rate applicable to the
tier in which such unit falls.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by
their duly authorized representatives.

FIRST AMENDMENT TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This First Amendment is made as of the 4th day of February, 2021. The Initial
Term set forth in Section 3.1 is hereby extended by a period of six (6)
months. In all other respects the Agreement is ratified and confirmed.

SECOND AMENDMENT TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Second Amendment is made as of the 5th day of September, 2021. Schedule A
is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following: For Annual
Volume of up to 15,000 million-messages, the Unit Price shall be $92.55 per
million-messages; for Annual Volume in excess of 15,000 million-messages and
up to 46,000 million-messages, the Unit Price shall be $89.59 per million-
messages; and for Annual Volume in excess of 46,000 million-messages, the Unit
Price shall be $86.75 per million-messages. The foregoing tiered Unit Prices
shall be applied on a marginal basis, such that each unit of Annual Volume
shall be priced at the rate applicable to the tier in which such unit falls.
In all other respects the Agreement is ratified and confirmed.
