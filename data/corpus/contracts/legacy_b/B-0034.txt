MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of March 12, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Halyard Networks Corporation ("Customer") and Bexley Communications, Ltd., a California corporation ("Vendor").

1. Services. Vendor shall provide unified communications and telephony services (the "Services") to Customer in accordance with this Agreement.

2.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months each, unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Termination for Convenience. Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.

3.1 Fees. Customer shall pay the fees set forth in Schedule A.

3.2 Fee Adjustments. Fees will increase by five percent (5%) on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

3.3 Minimum Commitment. There is no minimum purchase commitment under this Agreement.

3.4 Payment Terms. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.1 Affiliates. Customer's Affiliates may purchase Services under this Agreement only with Vendor's prior written consent.

5.1 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent. If Customer undergoes a change of control, Vendor may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

6.1 Exclusivity. Nothing in this Agreement obligates Customer to purchase the Services exclusively from Vendor.

6.2 Pricing Protection. Vendor represents that the unit prices in Schedule A are no less favorable than those offered to any similarly situated customer purchasing comparable volumes.

7. Confidentiality. Each party's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

7. Indemnification. Vendor will indemnify Customer, its Affiliates, and their respective officers and directors against third-party claims arising from Vendor's breach of this Agreement.

7. Limitation of Liability. Each party's total liability will not exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This Section states each party's exclusive remedy for such claims.

7. Service Levels. Vendor will maintain 99.9% availability and will issue service credits of ten percent (10%) of the monthly fees for any month in which availability falls below 99.5%.

7. Notices. Notices are deemed given five (5) days after mailing.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict of law principles.

SCHEDULE A - PRICING
Unit: user-month
Tier 1: 0 to 200,000 user-month per year at $17.22 per user-month
Tier 2: 200,000 to 600,000 user-month per year at $16.33 per user-month
Tier 3: Above 600,000 user-month per year at $15.40 per user-month
Tiered pricing applies on an all-units basis: the rate for the tier in which total Annual Volume falls applies to all units.
