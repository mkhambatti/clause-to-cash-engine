MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into
effective as of the 10th day of October, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and
between Marlow Satcom, Inc., a New York corporation ("Vendor"), and Halyard
Digital Ventures LLC ("Customer").

WHEREAS, Vendor is in the business of providing transponder capacity and
affiliate distribution services; and WHEREAS, Customer wishes to obtain such
services; NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

3.1 Initial Term. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective
Date and, unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, shall
continue for a period of two (2) years (the "Initial Term").

3.2 No Automatic Renewal. This Agreement shall expire at the end of the
Initial Term and shall not be renewed except by written instrument executed by
both Parties.

3.3 Termination Without Cause. Customer shall have the right, exercisable at
any time upon not less than sixty (60) days' written notice, to terminate this
Agreement without cause; provided that Customer shall pay Vendor an early
termination charge equal to one hundred percent (100%) of the fees payable for
the balance of the then-current term.

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in Schedule A.

4.2 Minimum Fees. Customer shall pay Vendor annual minimum fees of USD
13,720,000 irrespective of actual usage (the "Minimum Commitment"), on a take-
or-pay basis.

4.3 Adjustments. Fees shall be adjusted annually, effective on each
anniversary of the Effective Date, by an increase of three percent (3%) over
the fees in effect for the preceding year.

4.4 Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days after
receipt thereof.

5.1 Affiliate Orders. Customer's Affiliates shall be entitled to order
Services hereunder on the terms and at the prices set forth herein, and the
volumes ordered by all such Affiliates shall be aggregated with Customer's
volumes for purposes of Schedule A.

6.1 Assignment. Neither Party shall assign or otherwise transfer this
Agreement, including by operation of law or in connection with a change of
control, without the prior written consent of the other Party, such consent
not to be unreasonably withheld.

6.3 Pricing Warranty. Vendor makes no representation or warranty regarding the
prices charged to other customers.

7.1 The obligations of confidentiality set forth herein shall survive for a
period of three (3) years following termination or expiration of this
Agreement.

7.2 Vendor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates
and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against any
third-party claims arising out of Vendor's breach hereof.

7.3 In no event shall either Party's aggregate liability exceed the fees paid
in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to
liability, which shall be the Parties' exclusive remedy.

7.4 Vendor shall maintain availability of 99.9% and shall credit Customer ten
percent (10%) of the monthly fees for each month in which availability is
below 99.5%.

7.5 Any notice shall be deemed given five (5) days after deposit in the mail.

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of England and Wales, without giving effect to
conflict of laws principles.

SCHEDULE A. PRICING. For Annual Volume of up to 260 transponder-month, the
Unit Price shall be $40,375 per transponder-month; for Annual Volume in excess
of 260 transponder-month and up to 780 transponder-month, the Unit Price shall
be $37,401 per transponder-month; and for Annual Volume in excess of 780
transponder-month, the Unit Price shall be $35,241 per transponder-month. The
foregoing tiered Unit Prices shall be applied on a marginal basis, such that
each unit of Annual Volume shall be priced at the rate applicable to the tier
in which such unit falls.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by
their duly authorized representatives.
