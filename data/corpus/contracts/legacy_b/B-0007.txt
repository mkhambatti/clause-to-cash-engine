MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into
effective as of the 13th day of February, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and
between Garnet AdStitch Inc., a New York corporation ("Vendor"), and Halyard
Networks Corporation ("Customer").

WHEREAS, Vendor is in the business of providing ad decisioning and stitching
services; and WHEREAS, Customer wishes to obtain such services; NOW,
THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

3.1 Initial Term. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective
Date and, unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, shall
continue for a period of five (5) years (the "Initial Term").

3.2 Renewal. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall be automatically
extended for successive periods of two (2) years (each, a "Renewal Term")
unless either Party gives the other written notice of its intention not to
renew not less than six (6) months prior to the expiration of the then-current
term.

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in Schedule A.

4.2 No Minimum. Customer is not subject to any minimum fee or volume
commitment.

4.3 Adjustments. Fees shall be adjusted annually, effective on each
anniversary of the Effective Date, by an increase of two percent (2%) over the
fees in effect for the preceding year.

4.4 Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days after
receipt thereof.

5.1 Affiliate Orders. Customer's Affiliates shall be entitled to order
Services hereunder on the terms and at the prices set forth herein, and the
volumes ordered by all such Affiliates shall be aggregated with Customer's
volumes for purposes of Schedule A.

6.1 Assignment. Neither Party shall assign or otherwise transfer this
Agreement, including by operation of law or in connection with a change of
control, without the prior written consent of the other Party, such consent
not to be unreasonably withheld.

6.2 Requirements. Nothing herein shall obligate Customer to purchase the
Services exclusively from Vendor.

6.3 Pricing Warranty. Vendor hereby represents and warrants that the prices
set forth in Schedule A are, and shall remain during the term, no less
favorable than the prices offered by Vendor to any similarly situated customer
for comparable volumes.

7.1 The obligations of confidentiality set forth herein shall survive for a
period of three (3) years following termination or expiration of this
Agreement.

7.2 Vendor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates
and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against any
third-party claims arising out of Vendor's breach hereof.

7.3 In no event shall either Party's aggregate liability exceed the fees paid
in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to
liability, which shall be the Parties' exclusive remedy.

7.4 Vendor shall maintain availability of 99.9% and shall credit Customer ten
percent (10%) of the monthly fees for each month in which availability is
below 99.5%.

7.5 Any notice shall be deemed given five (5) days after deposit in the mail.

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to
conflict of laws principles.

SCHEDULE A. PRICING. For Annual Volume of up to 240,000 million-impressions,
the Unit Price shall be $41.09 per million-impressions; for Annual Volume in
excess of 240,000 million-impressions and up to 710,000 million-impressions,
the Unit Price shall be $39.71 per million-impressions; and for Annual Volume
in excess of 710,000 million-impressions, the Unit Price shall be $36.67 per
million-impressions. The foregoing tiered Unit Prices shall be applied on a
marginal basis, such that each unit of Annual Volume shall be priced at the
rate applicable to the tier in which such unit falls.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by
their duly authorized representatives.
