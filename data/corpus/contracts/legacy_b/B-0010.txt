MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into
effective as of the 16th day of February, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and
between Bexley Identity Systems Inc., a New York corporation ("Vendor"), and
Halyard Networks Corporation ("Customer").

WHEREAS, Vendor is in the business of providing consumer sign-in and identity
management services; and WHEREAS, Customer wishes to obtain such services;
NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

3.1 Initial Term. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective
Date and, unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, shall
continue for a period of four (4) years (the "Initial Term").

3.2 No Automatic Renewal. This Agreement shall expire at the end of the
Initial Term and shall not be renewed except by written instrument executed by
both Parties.

3.3 Termination Without Cause. Customer shall have the right, exercisable at
any time upon not less than one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice, to
terminate this Agreement without cause; provided that Customer shall pay
Vendor an early termination charge equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the
fees payable for the balance of the then-current term.

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in Schedule A.

4.2 No Minimum. Customer is not subject to any minimum fee or volume
commitment.

4.4 Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days
after receipt thereof.

5.1 Affiliate Orders. Affiliates of Customer may order Services hereunder
solely with the prior written approval of Vendor.

6.1 Assignment. Either Party may, without the consent of the other Party,
assign this Agreement in its entirety to an Affiliate or to any successor to
all or substantially all of its business or assets, whether by merger,
consolidation or otherwise.

6.2 Requirements. During the term of this Agreement, Customer shall purchase
exclusively from Vendor all of Customer's requirements for the Services.

6.3 Pricing Warranty. Vendor hereby represents and warrants that the prices
set forth in Schedule A are, and shall remain during the term, no less
favorable than the prices offered by Vendor to any similarly situated customer
for comparable volumes.

7.1 The obligations of confidentiality set forth herein shall survive for a
period of three (3) years following termination or expiration of this
Agreement.

7.2 Vendor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates
and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against any
third-party claims arising out of Vendor's breach hereof.

7.3 In no event shall either Party's aggregate liability exceed the fees paid
in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to
liability, which shall be the Parties' exclusive remedy.

7.4 Vendor shall maintain availability of 99.9% and shall credit Customer ten
percent (10%) of the monthly fees for each month in which availability is
below 99.5%.

7.5 Any notice shall be deemed given five (5) days after deposit in the mail.

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of the State of California, without giving effect to
conflict of laws principles.

SCHEDULE A. PRICING. For Annual Volume of up to 92,000,000 MAU-month, the Unit
Price shall be $0.0222 per MAU-month; for Annual Volume in excess of
92,000,000 MAU-month and up to 280,000,000 MAU-month, the Unit Price shall be
$0.0210 per MAU-month; and for Annual Volume in excess of 280,000,000 MAU-
month, the Unit Price shall be $0.0198 per MAU-month. The foregoing tiered
Unit Prices shall be applied on a volume-discount basis, such that the Unit
Price applicable to the tier in which total Annual Volume falls shall apply to
all units of Annual Volume.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by
their duly authorized representatives.

FIRST AMENDMENT TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This First Amendment is made as of the 21st day of November, 2025. Schedule A
is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following: For Annual
Volume of up to 92,000,000 MAU-month, the Unit Price shall be $0.0199 per MAU-
month; for Annual Volume in excess of 92,000,000 MAU-month and up to
280,000,000 MAU-month, the Unit Price shall be $0.0188 per MAU-month; and for
Annual Volume in excess of 280,000,000 MAU-month, the Unit Price shall be
$0.0177 per MAU-month. The foregoing tiered Unit Prices shall be applied on a
volume-discount basis, such that the Unit Price applicable to the tier in
which total Annual Volume falls shall apply to all units of Annual Volume. In
all other respects the Agreement is ratified and confirmed.
