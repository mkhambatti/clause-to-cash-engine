MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into
effective as of the 11th day of January, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and
between Juniper Legal LLC, a New York corporation ("Vendor"), and Halyard
Networks Corporation ("Customer").

WHEREAS, Vendor is in the business of providing legal document hosting and
review platform services; and WHEREAS, Customer wishes to obtain such
services; NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

3.1 Initial Term. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective
Date and, unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, shall
continue for a period of three (3) years (the "Initial Term").

3.2 Renewal. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall be automatically
extended for successive periods of two (2) years (each, a "Renewal Term")
unless either Party gives the other written notice of its intention not to
renew not less than one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the expiration of
the then-current term.

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in Schedule A.

4.2 No Minimum. Customer is not subject to any minimum fee or volume
commitment.

4.3 Adjustments. The fees set forth in Schedule A shall remain fixed during
the Initial Term.

4.4 Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days
after receipt thereof.

5.1 Affiliate Orders. Affiliates of Customer may order Services hereunder
solely with the prior written approval of Vendor.

6.1 Assignment. Either Party may, without the consent of the other Party,
assign this Agreement in its entirety to an Affiliate or to any successor to
all or substantially all of its business or assets, whether by merger,
consolidation or otherwise.

6.2 Requirements. Nothing herein shall obligate Customer to purchase the
Services exclusively from Vendor.

7.1 The obligations of confidentiality set forth herein shall survive for a
period of three (3) years following termination or expiration of this
Agreement.

7.2 Vendor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates
and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against any
third-party claims arising out of Vendor's breach hereof.

7.3 In no event shall either Party's aggregate liability exceed the fees paid
in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to
liability, which shall be the Parties' exclusive remedy.

7.4 Vendor shall maintain availability of 99.9% and shall credit Customer ten
percent (10%) of the monthly fees for each month in which availability is
below 99.5%.

7.5 Any notice shall be deemed given five (5) days after deposit in the mail.

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to
conflict of laws principles.

SCHEDULE A. PRICING. For Annual Volume of up to 210,000 GB-month, the Unit
Price shall be $12.96 per GB-month; for Annual Volume in excess of 210,000 GB-
month and up to 620,000 GB-month, the Unit Price shall be $12.42 per GB-month;
and for Annual Volume in excess of 620,000 GB-month, the Unit Price shall be
$11.56 per GB-month. The foregoing tiered Unit Prices shall be applied on a
marginal basis, such that each unit of Annual Volume shall be priced at the
rate applicable to the tier in which such unit falls.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by
their duly authorized representatives.

FIRST AMENDMENT TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This First Amendment is made as of the 18th day of July, 2025. The Initial
Term set forth in Section 3.1 is hereby extended by a period of one (1) year.
In all other respects the Agreement is ratified and confirmed.

SECOND AMENDMENT TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Second Amendment is made as of the 31st day of December, 2025. Schedule A
is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following: For Annual
Volume of up to 210,000 GB-month, the Unit Price shall be $11.39 per GB-month;
for Annual Volume in excess of 210,000 GB-month and up to 620,000 GB-month,
the Unit Price shall be $10.91 per GB-month; and for Annual Volume in excess
of 620,000 GB-month, the Unit Price shall be $10.15 per GB-month. The
foregoing tiered Unit Prices shall be applied on a marginal basis, such that
each unit of Annual Volume shall be priced at the rate applicable to the tier
in which such unit falls. In all other respects the Agreement is ratified and
confirmed.
