Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 9th February 2024 between Northgate Protective Services, Inc. ("we", "us") and Halyard Broadcasting Group, Inc. ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: uniformed officers at our lots and offices.

The relationship runs for one year counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least three months before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 100,000 guard-hour cost $37.27 each, the next 210,000 guard-hour cost $35.55 each, and anything beyond 310,000 guard-hour costs $32.73 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$2.34 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices go up 3% every year on the anniversary.

Invoices are due 30 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can only buy under this deal if we agree first.

The contract binds both sides and whoever follows them.

The Customer agrees to buy all its needs for this service from us and nobody else.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

California law governs.

Signed by both sides.
