Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 26th November 2025 between Northgate Payments Limited ("we", "us") and Halyard Networks Corporation ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: charging subscribers' cards each month.

The relationship runs for three years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 120 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another two years.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 12,000,000 transaction cost $0.2909 each, the next 25,000,000 transaction cost $0.2764 each, and anything beyond 37,000,000 transaction costs $0.2605 each.

There is no spend floor.

Prices go up 3.5% every year on the anniversary.

Invoices are due 30 days after receipt.

Only the Customer itself may use this arrangement; its sister companies cannot.

The contract cannot be transferred, including to an acquirer, unless the other side agrees.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We promise the prices here are as good as any comparable client gets.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

England and Wales law governs.

Signed by both sides.
