Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 9th September 2020 between Valence Talent Solutions, LLC ("we", "us") and Halyard Broadcasting Group, Inc. ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: supplying short-term workers on demand.

The relationship runs for one year counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least six months before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

The Customer can walk away early on 30 days' notice, but owes half of what is left on the commitment.

Pricing steps down with volume, applied to everything: up to 470,000 hour a year each hour costs $44.96; once volume passes 470,000 every hour is repriced at $42.32; past 1,400,000 it drops to $40.56 for every hour.

There is no spend floor.

Invoices are due 45 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can only buy under this deal if we agree first.

The contract cannot be transferred, including to an acquirer, unless the other side agrees.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

California law governs.

Signed by both sides.
