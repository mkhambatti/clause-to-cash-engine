Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 9th July 2024 between Solstice Render Cloud LLC ("we", "us") and Halyard Networks Corporation ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: crunching frames for effects shots on a big pool of cores.

The relationship runs for five years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 60 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Neither side gets to cancel early without cause.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 200,000,000 core-hour cost $0.0580 each, the next 390,000,000 core-hour cost $0.0553 each, and anything beyond 590,000,000 core-hour costs $0.0536 each.

Prices go up 2% every year on the anniversary.

Invoices are due 30 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can only buy under this deal if we agree first.

The contract cannot be transferred, including to an acquirer, unless the other side agrees.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

New York law governs.

Signed by both sides.
