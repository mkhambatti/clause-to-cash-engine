MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of January 18, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Halyard Broadcasting Group, Inc. ("Customer") and Umbra Broadcast Services Limited, a California corporation ("Vendor").

1. Services. Vendor shall provide channel playout and master control services (the "Services") to Customer in accordance with this Agreement.

2.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal. This Agreement will not renew automatically; any renewal requires a written amendment signed by both parties.

2.3 Termination for Convenience. Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.

3.1 Fees. Customer shall pay the fees set forth in Schedule A.

3.2 Fee Adjustments. Fees will increase by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

3.3 Minimum Commitment. Customer commits to purchase not less than $8,720,000 of Services in each contract year (the "Annual Minimum Commitment"). Any shortfall will be invoiced at the end of the contract year.

3.4 Payment Terms. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days of receipt.

5.1 Assignment. Either party may assign this Agreement without the other party's consent to an Affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets.

6.1 Exclusivity. Nothing in this Agreement obligates Customer to purchase the Services exclusively from Vendor.

6.2 Pricing Protection. Vendor represents that the unit prices in Schedule A are no less favorable than those offered to any similarly situated customer purchasing comparable volumes.

7. Confidentiality. Each party's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

7. Indemnification. Vendor will indemnify Customer, its Affiliates, and their respective officers and directors against third-party claims arising from Vendor's breach of this Agreement.

7. Limitation of Liability. Each party's total liability will not exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This Section states each party's exclusive remedy for such claims.

7. Service Levels. Vendor will maintain 99.9% availability and will issue service credits of ten percent (10%) of the monthly fees for any month in which availability falls below 99.5%.

7. Notices. Notices are deemed given five (5) days after mailing.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.

SCHEDULE A - PRICING
Unit: channel-month
Tier 1: 0 to 710 channel-month per year at $8,810 per channel-month
Tier 2: 710 to 2,100 channel-month per year at $8,488 per channel-month
Tier 3: Above 2,100 channel-month per year at $7,842 per channel-month
Tiered pricing applies on an all-units basis: the rate for the tier in which total Annual Volume falls applies to all units.
