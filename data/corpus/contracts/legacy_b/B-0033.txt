Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 13th January 2021 between Solstice Workplace Software, Ltd. ("we", "us") and Halyard Digital Ventures LLC ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: office apps and device management for staff.

The relationship runs for two years counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 90 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

The Customer can walk away early on 30 days' notice, but owes three quarters of what is left on the commitment.

Pricing steps down as volume grows: the first 290,000 user-month cost $28.52 each, the next 590,000 user-month cost $26.58 each, and anything beyond 880,000 user-month costs $25.56 each.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$9.96 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Invoices are due 45 days after receipt.

The contract cannot be transferred unless the other side agrees, and if the Customer is bought by someone else we may end the deal on 30 days' notice.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

New York law governs.

Signed by both sides.
