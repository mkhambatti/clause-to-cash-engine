Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 10th February 2021 between Arden People Systems Limited ("we", "us") and Halyard Digital Ventures LLC ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: software that runs paychecks and employee records.

The relationship runs for one year counting from signature.

Unless one side tells the other at least 90 days before the anniversary that it wants out, the deal rolls over for another year.

Pricing steps down with volume, applied to everything: up to 210,000 employee-month a year each employee-month costs $12.97; once volume passes 210,000 every employee-month is repriced at $11.95; past 620,000 it drops to $11.56 for every employee-month.

There is no spend floor.

Invoices are due 60 days after receipt.

Sister companies of the Customer can also buy at these rates, and their volume counts toward the tiers.

If either side is acquired or merges, the contract simply moves to the new owner.

The Customer remains free to use other suppliers.

We make no promises about what other clients pay.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

New York law governs.

Signed by both sides.
