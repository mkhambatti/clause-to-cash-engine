Services Agreement

This agreement is signed on 9th November 2023 between Dunmore Relationship Software, Inc. ("we", "us") and Halyard Digital Ventures LLC ("the Customer"). The service, in plain words: tracking advertiser and distributor relationships in the vendor's app.

The relationship runs for three years counting from signature.

There is no evergreen: if the parties want to keep going after the initial period they will have to sign something new.

The Customer can walk away early on 30 days' notice, but owes half of what is left on the commitment.

Pricing steps down with volume, applied to everything: up to 22,000 user-month a year each user-month costs $116.74; once volume passes 22,000 every user-month is repriced at $109.10; past 66,000 it drops to $100.84 for every user-month.

The Customer is on the hook for at least US$2.01 million a year whether or not it uses that much.

Prices are locked for the whole relationship.

Invoices are due 30 days after receipt.

The contract binds both sides and whoever follows them.

We promise the prices here are as good as any comparable client gets.

Confidential information stays confidential for three years after the agreement ends.

Each side's liability is capped at twelve months of fees and that is the exclusive remedy.

We will credit 10% of the monthly fee if uptime drops below 99.5%.

The Customer indemnifies us and our affiliates against claims arising from its content.

England and Wales law governs.

Signed by both sides.
